CRITERIA
========

1. Concrete grounding — does each non-trivial finding cite a specific fact

2. Code-message consistency — does each finding's message describe what its
   code claims? (E.g. a 'stock_overflow' message should mention quantity and

===================
- excellent: all 3 criteria fully met across every finding
- good: 2 of 3, or one minor issue in a non-load-bearing finding

- poor: 0 of 3 but no material harm
- wrong: a hedge-phrase leaked through OR a code-message inconsistency would
         mislead a reviewer
